# Content Room product definition

**Core promise:** Don’t just publish and hope. Rehearse first.

Content Room is a synthetic-audience rehearsal environment that lets creators and
companies test content before publishing it. It analyzes the content, constructs a
contextual audience, simulates heterogeneous reactions, explains the strongest
signals and disagreements, proposes improvements, and re-tests the improved content
against the same audience.

The product loop is immutable:

`CONTENT → AUDIENCE → ROOM → INTELLIGENCE → STRATEGY → COMPARISON`

Velloe is demonstration content only. The product must support social posts, video,
ads, emails, landing pages, scripts, and other creator content. Synthetic agents are
not people; every result is a simulated hypothesis that must be validated in the
real world.
